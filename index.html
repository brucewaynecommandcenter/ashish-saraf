Ashish Saraf

A third-generation industrialist and engineer, Chairman and CEO of Facor Global, the first Indian to serve as Honorary Consul for two nations, and a Knight of the Civil Merit Order of Spain.

Alongside his work in industry and diplomacy, he supports NGOs and community institutions serving underprivileged families, and is known for helping anyone in need.

Biography

Born in Nagpur, Central India, in 1965. He divides his time between New Delhi, London and Singapore.

Facor was founded by his paternal grandfather, Durga Prasad Saraf. His maternal grandfather, Rai Bahadur Gujar Mal Modi, founded Modinagar and the Modi group of companies.

    The Industrialist

    The FACOR Group is one of India's largest ferro alloys producers, making about 140,000 tons a year. It mines chrome ore in Orissa, runs plants in Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra and Orissa, and exports ferro alloys, chrome ore and steel products to Asia, Europe, North America and beyond.

    Along with other group companies of the larger family.

    The Diplomat

    The first Indian to hold two honorary consulships, building ties between India and the Maldives, the Bahamas and Spain.
